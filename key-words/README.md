# PLE Physics, Engineering, and Mathematics Keywords

This folder preserves a **page-ordered extraction** of technical terms, mathematical formulas, benchmark results, and real engineering contexts from the 20-page paper *PLE: Physical-Engineering's Last Exam*.

## Main extraction

[**Complete Physics, Engineering, and Mathematics Extraction**](PLE_PHYSICS_ENGINEERING_MATHEMATICS_EXTRACTION.md)

- Numbered terminology: **313 sequential keywords/concepts**, spanning pages 1–20 and the references.
- Formula register: the paper's two numbered equations, the associated cost objective, the feasibility gate and evaluation notation.
- Discipline coverage: structural, civil, electrical, mechanical and multidisciplinary engineering.
- Context: real engineering assignments, technical drawings, constraints, computer-aided design, optimization, verification, and experimental results.
- Provenance: source publication arXiv:2610.30537v2 (30 Oct 2026), by Rujun Han et al.

## Source and boundaries

- [Original scientific paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30537)
- [Official PLE benchmark repository](https://github.com/google-research/ple)
- [29 public development tasks](https://github.com/google-research/ple/blob/main/eval/dev_set.yaml)

The extraction is an **explanatory study note, not a verbatim copy of the scientific paper**. It distinguishes explicit equations from physical principles and engineering equations the paper names but does not derive. Values such as study scores, workload and cost are the **authors' reported results**, not outcomes of this repository's agent.

To prevent confusion, do not infer from this document that physical equations, standards, engineering models or verification algorithms have already been implemented or validated in the agent. See [architecture status](../docs/ARCHITECTURE_AND_GATES.md) and [results status](../results/STATUS.md).
